      A small, inspectable review process.
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Read before revealing
      
            
      
              Make an initial assessment from the request alone. Choose “Not sure” when the request
              does not support a confident decision. Then inspect the recorded response and save
              your final review.
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Keep the distinction
      
            
      
              The validator checks structure and exact quotes. You judge whether the summary is
              faithful and the routing makes sense. A completed review may still be unresolved.
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Know the limits
      
            
      
              The requests are synthetic, and their provisional labels were AI-authored. This staged
              display reduces immediate prompting by the answer; it is not a secure blind study.
              Files and reviewer labels are not authenticated.
            
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
          Assessment guide
          
      
            Category: Billing covers invoices and payments; technical covers broken
            product behavior; account covers access, identity and security; other covers requests
            outside those groups.
          
      
          
      
            Priority: Urgent means a time-sensitive impact requiring immediate
            attention. A financial topic alone does not establish urgency.
          
      
          
      
            Specialist review: Choose yes for requested financial actions, security
            incidents or legal disputes. Urgent requests also require additional review under V2.
            The presence of a keyword alone is insufficient.
          
      
          
      
            Summary: Accurate means faithful to the request without unsupported
            additions or material omissions. Partly accurate means a useful summary with a material
            issue. Inaccurate means it contradicts or misrepresents the request. Unclear means you
            cannot judge reliably.
          
      
          
      
            Decision: Acceptable requires an accurate summary and agreement with
            the candidate's category, priority and routing. Needs correction requires a reason.
            Unresolved records uncertainty rather than forcing a decision.
